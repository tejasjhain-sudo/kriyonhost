Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before launching your Minecraft server with KryonHost.

How many players can my server handle?
All plans include unlimited player slots — you set the max in your server.properties. As a rough guide: 2GB (Dirt) handles up to ~10–15 players on vanilla/Paper, 4GB (Stone) handles ~30 players, 6GB (Iron) handles ~50 players comfortably, 8GB (Diamond) handles 80–100 players on optimised Paper/Purpur, and the 12–16GB plans (Netherite/Obsidian) are designed for heavy modpacks and large SMPs of 100+ concurrent players.
Can I run heavy modpacks like ATM9 or RLCraft?
Yes. ATM9 (All The Mods 9) typically needs a minimum of 10–12GB RAM to run well. We recommend the Netherite (12GB) or Obsidian (16GB) plan for 400+ mod packs. RLCraft can run on 6–8GB. The Ryzen 9 7950X's high single-core clock means mod overhead is processed faster than on any EPYC or Xeon-based Minecraft host.
Which server software do you support?
Our one-click installer supports Vanilla (all versions), Paper, Purpur, Fabric, Forge, NeoForge, and Bedrock/Geyser for crossplay. Popular modpacks like ATM9 and RLCraft are also available. You can also upload any custom server JAR or modpack ZIP directly via FTP or the web file manager.
What does "sub-10ms India ping" actually mean?
Our Mumbai (MUM-1) node has direct peering with Airtel, Jio, BSNL, and Tata Communications backbone networks. Players in Mumbai, Pune, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Chennai typically see 3–8ms ping. Players in other Indian cities typically see 8–20ms. This results in crisp, responsive PvP with no rubber-banding.
How does the DDoS protection work for Minecraft?
Our Cryo L7 Minecraft filter understands the Minecraft protocol at a deep level. It detects and drops fake player handshakes, bot-flood connections, malformed packet streams, and volumetric UDP floods before they ever reach your server process. This means 0% CPU impact on your JVM — your TPS stays locked at 20.0 even under active attacks. The network capacity behind this is 500+ Tbps of Anycast scrubbing.
How do backups work? Can I restore a corrupted world?
Automated daily backups are taken off-node every 24 hours. You can restore any backup to a clean state in one click from your control dashboard — the process takes under 30 seconds. If a Creeper destroyed spawn, a plugin corrupted your world, or you just want to roll back — we've got you covered.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from the billing dashboard. When upgrading, your world data is preserved and the new resources are applied with a quick server restart. When downgrading, make sure your world size fits within the new plan's storage allocation.
Is there a lock-in contract or setup fee?
No. All Minecraft plans are month-to-month with zero setup fees. You can cancel at any time and your server stays active until the end of the current billing period. We don't believe in trapping you — we keep you by being the best host for Indian Minecraft communities.
